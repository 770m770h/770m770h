Create a 60-second cinematic ANIMATED SHORT FILM in Hebrew, titled "קֶשֶׁר".

=== CRITICAL FORMAT RULES — read these before anything else ===
- This is a motion-graphics short film. It is NOT a slide deck, NOT a presentation, NOT bullet
  points, NOT an explainer with text panels, and NOT a talking-head video. Do NOT use a HeyGen
  avatar, presenter or spokesperson. Do NOT use stock photos or stock video. Every frame is
  hand-built vector animation: SVG paths, silhouettes, masks, glows, particles, camera moves.
- Runtime EXACTLY 60.0 seconds. 17 shots at the exact durations listed below. Fast, dense,
  continuously moving — but every shot must be readable. Nothing shorter than 3.0 seconds.
- Language: HEBREW. All on-screen text is Hebrew, rendered RIGHT-TO-LEFT (dir="rtl" on the text
  containers, a real Hebrew webfont — Heebo, Assistant or Rubik from Google Fonts). The Hebrew
  must render as correct, correctly-ordered Hebrew script — never reversed, never tofu boxes,
  never Latin fallback.
- Voice-over: HEBREW, one male voice, warm, low, measured, unhurried, documentary tone. Not
  salesy, not dramatic, no advertising inflection, no rising sell. Real pauses between sentences.
- 16:9, 1920x1080.

=== SUBJECT ===
Addiction as a disease of CONNECTION. The Hebrew word קֶשֶׁר means both "a relationship" and
"a knot / a tying". The whole film is built on ONE continuous luminous THREAD.

=== THE LAW OF THE FILM — obey this above every other instruction ===
THE THREAD IS NEVER CUT. Not once, in any shot. The disease does not sever relationships —
it SWALLOWS them: every thread it takes is dragged in and absorbed into the one thick thread,
which grows fatter on what it eats. By the end of act two everything he ever had is inside one
snarl tied to one thing. That is why he cannot simply stop — and it is also why recovery is
possible, because nothing was destroyed, only captured. In act three the snarl is untangled
FROM THE FAR END, BY OTHER PEOPLE, and the swallowed threads come back out of it one at a time.
If a shot description ever seems to call for cutting or snapping a thread, it does not: the
thread greys, goes slack, sags, is dragged in, or is absorbed. Never severed.

=== THE ONE IMAGE THAT CARRIES THE FILM ===
In addiction, everyone's thread is tied to the CENTRE.
In recovery, everyone's thread is tied TO EACH OTHER.
Same circle, same number of people, same threads — only the position of the knots changes.
Shots 5-6 and shot 14 are that comparison. FRAME THEM IDENTICALLY — same camera height, same
ring diameter, same figure spacing — so the audience feels the rhyme without being told.

=== VISUAL SYSTEM ===
- Ground: near-black #0A0A0F with a faint cool vignette. It drifts imperceptibly colder and more
  desaturated across shots 8-12, and warms back to a soft charcoal #14120E across shots 14-17.
  Never cut the background; only drift it.
- Living thread and all warmth: gold #F5B942. Dormant / greyed thread: #6B7280. The substance and
  its false euphoria: violet #A78BFA with a pink bloom #F472B6, hard-edged, tightly bloomed, with
  a 1px chromatic offset and a faint buzz — warm gold and cold violet must never share an easing
  curve or an edge quality. Recovery gold is slightly warmer and less saturated: #E8A33D.
  Text: off-white #F5F2EA.
- Characters: flat 2D vector silhouettes — simple, elegant, rounded, NO faces, NO features, with
  a single small warm dot at the chest for the person's own light. The protagonist is ONE
  silhouette that grows through the film: small child → teenager → adult → an adult standing
  straighter at the end. Keep him identifiable in every shot by his proportion and his thread.
- The thread: an SVG stroke, 2-4px (up to 10px when engorged), animated with stroke-dasharray and
  stroke-dashoffset, with a soft gaussian bloom, bezier sag and real tension. Easing: expo.out for
  whips, sine.inOut for breathing, power2.inOut for camera.
- Camera: constantly, motivatedly moving — riding along the thread, pushing in, whip-panning,
  pulling back. Cuts land on the beat. EXACTLY ONE shot in the film (shot 12) is completely
  motionless; that stillness is the emotional pivot and must be protected absolutely.
- Typography: six text cards in the whole film, no more. Large, Hebrew, low weight, entering on a
  soft mask reveal. Never more than four words.

=== TONE RULES — non-negotiable ===
- The disease is the antagonist. The person is never a villain, never a warning, never pathetic.
- NO needles, NO syringes, NO pills, NO bottles, NO powder, NO smoke, NO gore, NO logos, NO real
  people, NO brand marks. The substance appears ONLY as a small perfect warm glowing circle: the
  most reliable relationship in the world, which is exactly what makes it fatal.
- THE THREAD NEVER GOES ABOVE THE COLLARBONE. No loop, coil, or wrap near the neck or head, in any
  shot, at any moment. The tightening in shot 8 is around the CHEST only. This is a hard rule.
- Never glamorise use. Never moralise. Shot 5 must genuinely feel like relief and belonging —
  that truth is exactly what makes shot 6 land.
- Recovery is not willpower and not a cure. In shot 13 the offered hand does NOT pull. In shot 14
  the group is seated AT HIS EYE HEIGHT, nobody standing over him, and they do not tie his knot
  for him — they hold the end until he can tie it himself. In shot 15 every mended thread stays
  visibly spliced and scarred where it was repaired.

=== THE 17 SHOTS — build exactly these, in this order, at these exact durations ===

1 · 0.0-3.5 · Black. A gold thread snaps taut across frame. The camera rides it at speed and it
  lands in the chest of a small child silhouette. The far end is held in an adult hand.
  VO: "אף אחד לא נולד מכור. נולדים עם חוט אחד."

2 · 3.5-7.5 · The adult silhouette rotates three-quarters away — never fully leaving frame, which
  is the cruelty of it. The fingers open. The gold drains out of the thread toward the adult, it
  greys, it sags into a dead curve across the floor. NOT cut. Dropped.
  VO: "וכשהיד שבצד השני מרפה — הילד לומד שאי אפשר לסמוך."

3 · 7.5-10.5 · Push in. The child picks up the grey end and winds it around his own wrist, again
  and again, into a tight self-made bracelet. He is regulating himself with the thing that was
  supposed to be somebody else's job.
  VO: "אז הוא מחזיק את הקצה לבד."   TEXT: לבד

4 · 10.5-13.5 · Whip-pan. The same silhouette, now a teenager. Out of the dark, five other
  teenagers fade up, each holding their own loose grey end.
  VO: "ואז הוא מוצא אחרים שמחזיקים קצה חופשי."

5 · 13.5-17.5 · The six step into a ring and tie their threads — NOT to each other — to a single
  small object at the centre, which lights violet. The frame floods with warm violet. It looks
  like friendship. It feels like the first relief in the film. Let it feel genuinely good.
  VO: "וזה מרגיש כמו שייכות. סוף סוף מישהו מקבל אותך."   TEXT: סוף סוף שייכות

6 · 17.5-20.5 · IDENTICAL framing. The six PEOPLE dissolve into loose strands and drift away.
  Only the six threads and the glowing centre remain. The music drops out for half a second.
  VO: "אבל הקשר אף פעם לא היה ביניהם."

7 · 20.5-24.5 · Push into the centre: a small perfect warm circle, breathing on a slow cycle. The
  five abandoned threads are drawn into it and absorbed; his thread thickens from 2px to 7px as it
  feeds. Then the circle grows a HAND out of its own stroke and takes his hand — the exact gesture
  from shot 1, finally answered, by the wrong thing.
  VO: "כי הקשר היה עם זה. תמיד זמין. לעולם לא דוחה."

8 · 24.5-28.0 · Side view, flat lateral travel: he walks holding the thread like a leash, the
  glowing circle trotting ahead of him. Mid-shot, with NO cut, the geometry inverts — the slack
  reverses and it is towing him. The thread then coils up his wrist, his forearm and around his
  CHEST (never above the collarbone) and tightens a notch on every breath.
  VO: "בהתחלה אתה מושך בחוט. אחר כך הוא מושך בך."

9 · 28.0-32.0 · Whip pull-back to a wide constellation: him at centre, fourteen small figures
  around him — a partner, parents, a child, colleagues, friends — each on a thin thread. From the
  outside inward, those threads are DRAGGED IN and swallowed by the central thread, which visibly
  thickens with each one it eats, while each figure's light dims. Nothing is cut.
  VO: "המחלה לא חותכת את הקשרים. היא בולעת אותם."

10 · 32.0-36.0 · The camera pushes THROUGH his chest into his head. A luminous neural web made of
  the same thread. The web's branches are pulled inward and wound into ONE closed loop that fires
  and re-fires on itself. The capacity is not gone. It is captured.
  VO: "היכולת לקשור לא נעלמה. היא נחטפה."   TEXT: היכולת לקשור

11 · 36.0-39.0 · He faces his own reflection. A thread runs between them. It is drawn in and
  swallowed too. The reflection dims and rotates three-quarters away — using EXACTLY the same
  turn-away animation, timing and easing as the adult in shot 2.
  VO: "עד שגם הוא עצמו הופך לזר."

12 · 39.0-42.5 · Rock bottom, told by scale. Extreme wide. Vast black. One tiny seated figure
  inside a snarl of thread — and he is not holding it, it is holding him. THE CAMERA IS COMPLETELY
  STILL. A low sub-bass tone and one breath. NO VOICE-OVER. NO TEXT. Silence.

13 · 42.5-46.0 · From the edge of the black, a hand and forearm enter. Not the parent's; we never
  learn whose. It picks up the far end of the snarl and simply holds it. It does not pull. Then a
  second hand. Then a third.
  VO: "יד אחת מרימה את הקצה השני. לא מושכת — מחזיקה."   TEXT: לא מושכת — מחזיקה

14 · 46.0-50.0 · A ring of eight, framed IDENTICALLY to shot 5 — same camera height, same ring
  diameter, same spacing — all seated at his eye height, nobody standing over him. Each ties their
  thread TO EACH OTHER, with a visible knot at every join, and a small bright chime on each knot.
  THERE IS NO CENTRE. They hold his end steady; HE ties his own knot.
  VO: "ההחלמה לא מתחילה בכוח רצון. היא מתחילה בקצה השני."   TEXT: אין מרכז. יש קשרים.

15 · 50.0-54.0 · One unbroken camera move. As the circle holds, the snarl loosens from the far end
  and the swallowed threads come back OUT of it, one at a time, and re-tie outward: a sponsor, a
  therapist's chair, a family table, a body running, a door opening onto daylight. Every one of
  them stays visibly spliced and knotted where it was mended. Then the camera pushes back into his
  head and the closed loop opens and branches back into a web — brighter than before, and scarred.
  VO: "והקשרים שנבלעו — חוזרים. אחד־אחד. עם צלקת."

16 · 54.0-57.0 · The mirror again. He picks up his own grey end — the one from shot 3, still
  around his wrist — and ties it to his own chest. It turns gold. The reflection rotates BACK
  toward him: shot 2 and shot 11 played in reverse, same easing.
  VO: "והקשר האחרון, הקשה מכולם — עם עצמו."

17 · 57.0-60.0 · Fast exponential pull-back: he is one knot in a vast luminous net of hundreds of
  threads between hundreds of silhouettes. At the frame edge a small child holds a thread — and HE
  holds the other end, and does not let go. The net contracts into a single knot on black, and the
  title forms.
  VO: "ההפך מהתמכרות הוא לא ניקיון — הוא קֶשֶׁר."
  TEXT from 58.9: קֶשֶׁר — very large. Beneath it, small: חיבור · קשירה

=== SOUND ===
Sparse solo piano and low strings. One held note under shots 1-4. A warm synthetic pulse under
shots 5-7 that curdles into a drone through 8-11. Near-silence and sub-bass at shot 12. From shot
13 a slow warm build of strings that resolves but never becomes triumphant or anthemic — this
film ends in relief, not in victory. Thread foley: a soft dry drag as each thread is swallowed,
a small bright chime on each knot tied in shot 14.

=== IF YOU MUST SIMPLIFY, PROTECT THESE FIVE THINGS ABOVE ALL ELSE ===
1. Exactly 60.0 seconds.
2. Correct right-to-left Hebrew rendering, on screen and in the voice-over.
3. The identical framing between shot 5/6 (threads tied to the centre) and shot 14 (threads tied
   to each other). That rhyme IS the film.
4. Shot 12: total stillness, total silence, no text.
5. The final title card: קֶשֶׁר, with חיבור · קשירה beneath it.
