# קֶשֶׁר — "KESHER"
### A 60-second animated short on addiction as a disease of connection

## The hook

In Hebrew, **קֶשֶׁר** means *relationship / connection* — and it shares its root with
*tying a knot*. The word for the thing we lose **is** the word for the thing we do
with a thread. The film's central metaphor is therefore not applied to the language;
it is already inside it. One word, one image, one film.

## Logline

A single luminous thread follows one person from the first bond of childhood to the
last bond of addiction and back again — because the disease is not about a substance,
it is about what happens to a thread when nobody holds the other end.

## The one image that carries the whole film

> **In addiction, everyone's thread ties to the CENTER.
> In recovery, everyone's thread ties to EACH OTHER.**

Same circle. Same people. Same threads. The only difference is where the knots are.
That single visual distinction is the entire clinical thesis, and it can be read in
half a second.

## The five stages, as thread states

| Stage | Thread state |
|---|---|
| 1. Early rupture in the bond with the parent | the thread is dropped; it frays and greys |
| 2. The child self-regulates alone | he winds the loose end around his own wrist |
| 3. "The friends of the drugs" | six torn ends tie to a glowing centre, not to each other |
| 4. The bond with the substance | one thread thickens, all others snap, it wraps his body |
| 5. Disconnection outward then inward | the constellation goes dark, then the neural web, then the reflection |
| — Rock bottom | one figure, one thread, and the thread is holding *him* |
| 6. Recovery | a thread is offered and waits; knots appear *between* people; the last knot is to himself |

## Rules the film obeys

- The disease is the antagonist. The person is never the villain, never pitiable, never a warning.
- No needles, no pills, no bottles, no gore. The substance is drawn as a small perfect
  warm circle — the most reliable relationship in the world, which is exactly why it is fatal.
- Recovery is **not** willpower and **not** a cure. Some threads stay visibly scarred after
  they are re-tied. The offered thread waits before it is taken.
- Every recovery beat is the precise reverse of a specific earlier beat, animated with the
  same motion played backwards, so the mirror is felt rather than explained.
