# קֶשֶׁר — the finished film, shot by shot (60.000s)

Seventeen shots. One thread. The thread is never cut, in any shot.
There is no spoken narration: every line below is Hebrew kinetic typography, set
right-to-left in Heebo, revealed on a mask and timed to be read, not skimmed.

## Score

Written to picture, in D minor. Sine pads and inharmonic bells for the ache; a warm
violet pulse for the false circle; a **half-second drop-out at 17.5s** on the reveal;
a minor second rubbing the tonic through the second act; a dry drag on each swallowed
thread; **near-silence and a 41 Hz sub at rock bottom**; three bells at 43.6 / 44.1 / 44.9
for the two failed catches and the one that holds; a bowed build from 46s that resolves
without ever becoming a fanfare.

## Rules the film obeys, verifiable frame by frame

- **The thread is never cut.** The disease swallows relationships and grows fatter on them.
  Nothing was destroyed — only captured. That is why recovery is possible at all.
- **The antagonist gets no easing.** Every tween on the centre is strictly linear, against
  the organic easing of every human element. The audience reads "this is not a person"
  twenty-five seconds before the film says it.
- **The thread never crosses above the collarbone.** No loop near a neck, in any frame.
- **Recovery is not done to him.** No warmth moves until his own fingers close.
- **No needles, pills, bottles, powder or smoke.** The substance is a small warm circle.
- **The mother is at capacity, not cruel** — her other hand is visibly full from shot 2,
  she is never in shadow and never fully turns.
